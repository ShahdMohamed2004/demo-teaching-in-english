Ready for an adventure?

Use a big screen or let children tap on a tablet. Choose any activity, then press Start activity. Follow the visible captions and give everyone time to answer.

Only optional star progress is stored locally. No accounts, recordings, drawing uploads, or AI analysis.

Listen together

Click “Let’s begin” to enable sound. Use an English voice and check device volume. Replay always repeats the exact current question. Mute stops voice and effects. Some browser voices require a network; installed voices can work offline.

Move at your pace

Auto advance starts off. Next is always teacher-controlled in Four corners and Draw and guess. Pause freezes the corners countdown and round. Relaxed mode removes the timer. Reveal shows an answer without earning a star.

Tap, drag, or use keys

In Match, tap an action, then a vehicle. You can also drag with a mouse or finger. With a keyboard, Tab to an action, press Enter or Space, then Tab to a vehicle and select it.

Keep the language clear

Car, bus, train, and motorbike use “drive” in this lesson. Plane uses “fly”; boat uses “float”. All have wheels except boat. Negative corners prompts name the vehicle, so there is only one answer.

A secret, then a drawing

A local random picker chooses a vehicle. Play the spoken clue without showing its picture. The child draws from listening or memory. “What is it?” gives three picture choices. The app never recognizes or analyzes drawings.

Celebrate each first win

One star per first correct question. Repeated taps, replay, and activity resets do not add duplicate stars. Revealed questions earn no stars. Reset all progress starts a fresh lesson.

The HTML download contains all seven activities, illustrations, and sounds. Open it directly in a modern browser. If speech is unavailable, use captions and a teacher’s voice.
